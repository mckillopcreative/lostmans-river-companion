The story

Fifteen-year-old Matt Creek spends the summer after his father’s death as his uncle Dan’s assistant fishing guide in Chokoloskee, at the top of the Ten Thousand Islands. There is a legendary tarpon, a smuggling ring, a houseboat, a storm, and a lot of snook.
