What every ad sells: a book-expansion experience, not the book. The novel on the reader's own tablet, read and read aloud, and with each chapter the real world it sends them into: the fish, the chart, the tides, the knots on real line, a field mission done off the screen and signed off by a grown-up, a paper Field Journal, a license at the end.

So in every image: a screen showing the app, plus real-world evidence (a knot, a tide table, a journal page, the license). The paperback appears only as a small emblem. Hands and backs only; never a child's face. The GPT direction that produces these images is ads-for-gpt/02-CREATIVE-DIRECTION-V2.md.

The run

No fixed opening date. The store opens the day Stripe is connected, and the ads start the same day. One campaign carries the launch; a second, smaller one carries the Christmas-gift angle in December.
