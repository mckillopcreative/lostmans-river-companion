# Creative direction, version 2: sell the experience, not the book

*This replaces the earlier briefs. Same attachments (cover, houseboat painting, lettering), same palette, sizes and rules as `00-AD-BRIEF.md`. Keep image **D (smugglers)** from your first set as it is. Re-make everything else to this direction.*

## What is being sold (read twice)

Not the paperback. A **book-expansion experience** for readers 10 to 15, on their own phone or tablet, that turns the novel into a summer as an assistant fishing guide in the Florida Everglades, like Matt, the main character:

- **On the screen:** the whole novel to read, and to hear read aloud, chapter by chapter; and with each chapter, the app's pages for that chapter: the characters, the fish caught (with facts), where they were caught on a real chart, the tide tables, the knots.
- **Off the screen, which is the point:** 23 field missions done for real and signed off by a grown-up: tie the knot on real line, read tomorrow's tide, draw a treasure map with a compass, pick up lost fishing line at a pond, plan the food for a three-day trip, a real fishing day. A printable Field Journal filled in by hand with a pencil. Three badges. A printed Assistant Fishing Guide license at the end.

So every image must show **both halves at once: a screen with the app, and the real world it sends the kid into.** The paperback is sold separately on Amazon; the cover may appear only as a small emblem (a corner, a thumbnail on the screen), never as the hero object, never wrapped, never held open.

## The visual rule

Every scene is built from three kinds of thing:

1. **A screen** (phone or tablet, plain, no brand) showing one page of the app in the site's own style: a hand-drawn chart with a route and an X; a painted fish card ("Common snook"); a knot diagram with numbered steps; a chapter heading ("Chapter 7 · A Tarpon") with a short paragraph; a missions list with check marks; a tide table.
2. **Real-world evidence:** a knot tied on real monofilament; a spool of line; a lure; a tide table printout; a pencil; printed Field Journal pages with handwriting and a sketched fish; the printed license with its three round badges (a snook, a tarpon, a ship's wheel); a compass; a hand-drawn treasure map; a rod; a dock.
3. **The place:** Everglades water, mangroves, a skiff, a houseboat, sunrise or dusk.

**People:** hands, shoulders, backs and silhouettes only. Never a child's face. A grown-up's hand signing a journal page is good; a parent's hand beside a kid's hand on a knot is good.

**Words on the image:** one headline, optionally a four-word icon row. Prefer the row **Read it · Hear it · Do it for real · Earn it** (icons: open book, headphones, hands tying a knot, a certificate with a ribbon). Spell **Lostman's** (one apostrophe) and **McKillop** exactly.
