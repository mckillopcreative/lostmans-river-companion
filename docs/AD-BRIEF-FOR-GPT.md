# Ad images for *Escape to Lostman's River*: brief for an image model

*November 2026. Five Facebook/Instagram ad images for a children's adventure novel and its companion experience. You decide composition and lighting. The words, the sizes and the rules below are fixed. Plan: `docs/CAMPAIGN-OPENING.md`.*

## The product
*Escape to Lostman's River* is a middle-grade novel (readers about 10 to 15) by **Linda and John McKillop, a mother and son**. Fifteen-year-old Matt spends a summer as an assistant fishing guide in the Florida Everglades (Chokoloskee Island, the Ten Thousand Islands): snook, tarpon, mangroves, a houseboat, smugglers. It now comes with the **Summer Fishing Guide Experience**: the book to read and hear, missions, real fishing skills done for real, a Field Journal and a license.

## Style (all five)
- **Painted, warm, authentic:** like the attached cover and the houseboat-at-dusk painting. Not cartoonish, not comic-book. Think a classic outdoor-adventure illustration with real light.
- **Palette:** paper `#f6f1e7`, ink `#1f2a2e`, mangrove green `#2f5d50`, gulf teal `#2a7f8e`, sunrise orange `#e8873a`, sky `#6ab8e8`.
- **Type:** a serif for headlines (like Source Serif or Georgia); a hand-lettered script only for small notes. The "Summer Fishing Guide Experience" lettering (attached) may appear.
- **Show the real book** (the attached new cover) in each image, upright and legible, as a physical paperback.
- **Sizes:** each at **1080 × 1350** (4:5 feed) and **1080 × 1920** (9:16 stories, with the top and bottom 250 px kept free of text and faces).
- **Text on the image:** short. Headline plus at most a 4-icon row. Meta favors images that are mostly picture.

## Never
- A child's face. Children may appear only from behind, in silhouette, or as hands.
- Prices, "free", "screen-free", star ratings, Amazon or Kindle logos, web addresses, QR codes.
- Any brand other than the book (no real tackle, boat or phone brands; a phone or tablet, if shown, has a blank or generic screen).
- Misspellings: it is **Lostman's** (one apostrophe, before the s) and **McKillop** (capital K).

## The five images

**A. "Give them a summer job in the Everglades."**
Sunrise over still water and mangroves; a flats skiff at a weathered dock. On the dock boards: the paperback, a pair of headphones, a short length of fishing line tied in a clinch knot, and a printed certificate titled "Assistant Fishing Guide". Headline at top. Icon row at bottom, four simple line icons with words: **Read it · Hear it · Tie it · Earn it.**

**B. The reluctant reader (no headline on the image; the quote goes in the ad text).**
Dusk on a dock. From behind or over the shoulder: a teenager's shoulders and hands, headphones on, the open paperback on their knees, a fishing rod leaning on the piling. Warm, quiet, unposed. No face.

**C. "The gift that lasts all summer."**
Close still life on a wooden table: the paperback wrapped in brown paper, tied with fishing line instead of ribbon, a small bright lure as the bow; beside it a printed gift card that says "Your summer at Lostman's River starts here." A Christmas-season warmth without Santa: a pine sprig, soft lights out of focus.

**D. "Smugglers. A giant tarpon. And he's fifteen."**
Night in the Ten Thousand Islands: a seaplane touching down on black water with no lights, a flashlight beam flashing from the mangroves, a small skiff hidden in the roots, a huge moon. Mysterious, not frightening (readers are 10 to 15). The paperback in the lower corner.

**E. "Can your kid earn this?"**
A printed certificate titled "Assistant Fishing Guide" with three round badges (a snook, a tarpon, a ship's wheel), lying on an open tackle box with lures and a coil of line. Late afternoon light.
